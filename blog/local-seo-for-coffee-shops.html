Local SEO for Cafés: 10 Moves That Bring in More Walk-Ins

“Coffee near me” is one of the most common local searches there is. Independent cafés can beat the chains in their neighborhood with a few focused moves.

Taploe TeamSep 28, 2026 · 5 min read

1. Pick precise categories

Choose “Coffee shop” as your primary category, then add relevant extras like “Café,” “Bakery” or “Breakfast restaurant” if they fit.

2. Keep hours obsessively accurate

Nothing loses a customer like arriving to a closed door. Update holiday hours early.

3. Post your menu

Add your menu with prices. Searchers want to know if you have oat milk before they walk over.

4. Show the space

Remote workers search for outlets, seating and natural light. Photograph them.

5. List attributes

Wi-Fi, outdoor seating, wheelchair accessibility, vegan options. Fill in every attribute that applies.

6. Make reviews effortless

Put a tap-to-review card by the pickup counter, where people wait with phones in hand.

7. Turn Wi-Fi into a touchpoint

Customers always ask for the Wi-Fi password. Put the review card right next to it.

8. Post weekly updates

New seasonal drinks, live music, a new pastry. Google posts keep your profile active.

9. Get listed locally

Neighborhood blogs, local guides and “best coffee in…” lists send both visitors and credibility.

10. Grow your Instagram from the counter

Latte art is made for Instagram. An Instagram tap card turns regulars into followers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rank for “coffee near me”?

Proximity matters most, but a complete profile, accurate hours, photos and steady reviews help you stand out among nearby options.

Should I post on Google Business Profile?

Yes. Weekly posts keep your listing fresh and give customers reasons to visit.

Where do review cards work best in a café?

At the pickup counter and next to the Wi-Fi details.
