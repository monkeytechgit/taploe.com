NFC vs QR Code Business Cards: What Is the Difference?

NFC and QR codes both send someone to your digital profile. They work differently, and the best cards use both.

In this guide
  1. How NFC sharing works
  2. How QR code sharing works
  3. Side by side
  4. Why you want both
  5. Static vs dynamic
  6. Next steps

How NFC sharing works

The person taps their phone on the card. The phone reads the chip and opens your link. It is fast and needs no camera. See how NFC business cards work.

How QR code sharing works

The person opens their camera and points it at the printed code. The phone recognizes it and offers to open the link. It works on virtually every modern smartphone.

Why you want both

A card with NFC and a QR code covers every phone and every situation. Tap when the phone supports it, scan when it does not, or share the QR from your own screen in a video call.

Static vs dynamic

A static QR code is fixed forever. A dynamic one points to a profile you can edit, so you never need to reprint. Taploe cards connect to an editable digital profile.

Frequently asked questions

Is NFC better than QR?

NFC is faster, QR is more universal. Cards with both give the best coverage.

Can I share my digital card without a physical card?

Yes. You can share your profile link or QR code from your phone.

Do QR codes on cards expire?

Not if they point to a permanent profile link that you control.
