NFC Business Cards for Sales Teams: Setup, Cost and Benefits

Sales teams live on first impressions and follow-up speed. Standardized smart cards make every rep look sharp and every lead easier to convert.

Taploe TeamSep 21, 2026 · 6 min read

Benefits for the business

  • Brand consistency. Every rep uses the same design and layout.
  • Central control. Update titles, phone numbers and offers in one place.
  • No more reprints when someone changes roles or leaves.
  • Faster follow-up. Contacts saved with one tap and booking links included.

Benefits for reps

  • A professional impression in every meeting.
  • Booking and demo links on the profile.
  • Easy sharing on calls: send the link or QR instantly.

Setup in 4 steps

  1. Choose a standard design and add your logo.
  2. Create a profile for each team member.
  3. Order cards in bulk.
  4. Roll out with a short training on how to tap and share.

Metal for leadership, PVC for everyone else

Many companies equip executives with metal cards and everyone else with standard NFC cards. See metal vs PVC.

Add review links

For service or local businesses, add your Google review link to every profile. See how to get more Google reviews.
