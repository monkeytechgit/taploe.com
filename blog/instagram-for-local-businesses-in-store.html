Instagram for Local Businesses: 9 Ideas That Turn Visitors Into Followers

Buying ads to reach people near your shop makes sense, but your warmest audience is already standing at your counter. These nine ideas help you turn foot traffic into followers who come back.

Taploe TeamOct 26, 2026 · 5 min read

1. Make following one tap

An Instagram NFC card by the register opens your profile instantly. No spelling out your handle.

2. Create a photo spot

A mural, neon sign or beautiful display gives people a reason to post and tag you.

3. Show the behind-the-scenes

Baking at 5 a.m., unboxing new stock, the team’s lunch. People follow people.

4. Feature customers (with permission)

Repost customers who tag you. It’s free social proof.

5. Announce things on Instagram first

New flavors, restocks, flash sales. Give followers a reason to stay.

6. Use Stories for daily specials

Stories are perfect for “today only” updates.

7. Put your handle on packaging

Bags, cups and receipts travel home with customers.

8. Collaborate with neighbors

Partner with the bakery or florist next door on a joint post or giveaway.

9. Ask at the right time

“If you liked it, we post new stuff every week on Instagram. Just tap here.”

Frequently asked questions

Can someone follow me without the Instagram app?

The tap opens your profile in the app if installed, or in the browser if not.

Where should an Instagram card go?

At checkout, by photo-worthy displays and on tables.

Can I run a giveaway for follows?

Instagram allows giveaways with clear rules, but don’t buy followers. Fake social proof is prohibited by the FTC’s review rule.
