How to Turn In-Store Visitors Into TikTok Followers

TikTok has become a discovery engine for local spots, from hidden-gem restaurants to quirky boutiques. Your in-store customers can help you grow there faster than any algorithm hack.

Taploe TeamSep 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Film what makes you different

The process, the people, the oddly satisfying moments. You don’t need fancy equipment, just a phone and good light.

Make following easy in person

Put a tap card by the counter that opens your TikTok profile. Customers who just had a great experience are your most likely followers.

Invite customers into the content

Taste tests, reactions and “rate my order” videos, always with permission.

Post consistently

A few short videos a week beats one big production a month.

Connect it to reviews

A viral video brings visitors. Make sure they find recent, strong reviews when they search for you. A Google review card helps keep them coming.

Frequently asked questions

Is TikTok worth it for small local businesses?

For visual businesses like food, beauty and retail, it can be a strong discovery channel.

Do I need to dance on TikTok?

No. Behind-the-scenes and process videos work great.

Can I film customers?

Only with their permission.
