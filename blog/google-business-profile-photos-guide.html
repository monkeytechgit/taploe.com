Google Business Profile Photos: What to Upload and How Often

Photos are often the first thing people look at on your Google listing, before your rating, before your hours. A profile with recent, real photos looks open and cared for. One with three blurry pictures from 2019 does not.

Taploe TeamOct 25, 2026 · 5 min read

The 8 photos every profile needs

  1. Logo: square, clean, readable small.
  2. Cover photo: your best wide shot.
  3. Exterior: so people recognize you from the street.
  4. Interior: what it feels like inside.
  5. Products or dishes: your bestsellers.
  6. Team: real people build trust.
  7. At work: the service in action.
  8. Common areas: parking, entrance, waiting room.

How often to upload

A few new photos a month is a good rhythm. Seasonal menus, new products, events and renovations are natural reasons to post.

Shoot like a customer

Use natural light, hold the phone level and avoid heavy filters. Authentic beats polished. Customers want to know what they’ll actually see.

Encourage customer photos

Reviews with photos are some of the most persuasive content on your profile. When you ask for a review, mention photos: “If you snapped a picture, feel free to add it!”

Mistakes to avoid

  • Stock photos that don’t show your business.
  • Text-heavy promotional graphics as photos.
  • Photos that are years out of date.

Frequently asked questions

Do photos help local ranking?

Google does not publish a photo ranking factor, but complete, active profiles tend to perform better and convert more viewers into visitors.

Can I remove bad customer photos?

You can report photos that violate Google’s policies. You can’t remove photos just because you dislike them.

What size should photos be?

Google recommends at least 720 x 720 pixels in JPG or PNG.
