Basics

  1. Claim and verify your profile.
  2. Use your real business name. Do not stuff keywords in.
  3. Choose the best primary category and add relevant secondary ones.
  4. Keep NAP consistent. Name, address and phone should match your website and other listings.
  5. Set accurate hours, including holidays.

Content

  1. Write a clear description covering what you offer and where.
  2. List services and products with descriptions.
  3. Add high-quality photos: exterior, interior, team and work examples.
  4. Post updates such as offers and news regularly.
  5. Add your website and booking link.

Reputation

  1. Collect reviews consistently. Use a direct link. See how to find your Google review link.
  2. Reply to every review. See how to respond to negative reviews.
  3. Answer questions in the Q&A section if available.

Website connection

  1. Add local content to your site: service pages and location details.
  2. Use structured data such as LocalBusiness markup with accurate information.

Turn visits into reviews

Reviews are one of the most controllable ranking factors. A Google review NFC card makes it effortless for customers to leave one at the point of purchase.
