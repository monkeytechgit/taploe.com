The 5-step response formula

  1. Wait a moment. Never reply while angry.
  2. Thank them. Acknowledge the feedback.
  3. Apologize where appropriate. Do not be defensive.
  4. Be specific. Show you read the review.
  5. Move offline. Give a contact for resolution.

Template 1: Service issue

"Hi [Name], thank you for sharing this. We are sorry your experience did not meet expectations. That is not the standard we aim for. Please contact us at [email/phone] so we can make this right."

Template 2: Long wait

"Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We apologize for the wait. We are reviewing our process to improve. We would appreciate the chance to make it up to you. Please reach out at [contact]."

Template 3: Unfair or inaccurate review

"Hi [Name], we take feedback seriously but could not find a record matching your description. Please contact us at [contact] with details so we can look into it."

Template 4: One-star with no comment

"Thank you for the rating, [Name]. We would love to understand how we can improve. Please contact us at [contact]."

What not to do

  • Do not argue or blame the customer.
  • Do not share private customer details.
  • Do not offer money to remove a review.

Flag fake reviews

If a review clearly violates Google policy, such as spam or a competitor attack, report it through your Business Profile.

Keep the balance

A few negative reviews look natural. The best defense is a steady flow of genuine positive ones. See 9 ways to get more Google reviews or use a Google review NFC card.
