Before the event

  • Update your profile with your current role, offer and a booking link.
  • Add a clear headshot so people recognize you afterwards.
  • Test your card on iPhone and Android.

During the event

  1. Introduce yourself in one sentence.
  2. Offer your card with a tap: "Tap your phone here to grab my details."
  3. Show the QR code as a backup for phones that do not tap.
  4. Note one thing about the conversation right away.

After the event

Because contacts have your profile, you can send a short follow-up message within 24 hours referencing your conversation and including your booking link. Speed matters more than polish.

Booth and trade show ideas

  • Display a large QR code that opens your company profile.
  • Give staff tap cards so every teammate shares consistent details. See Taploe for teams.

Stand out

A metal NFC card is memorable in a stack of paper cards. For volume, the standard NFC business card works well. Compare types in metal vs PVC NFC cards.
