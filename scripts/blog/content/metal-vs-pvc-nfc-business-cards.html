Do metal cards work with NFC?

Metal blocks radio signals, so a well-made metal NFC card is designed so that the chip and its antenna still read reliably. Quality matters here: a badly built metal card can be hard to scan. Choose a supplier that tests each design.

When to choose PVC

  • You are ordering for a whole team or a large event.
  • You want full-color printing and quick turnaround.
  • You want the lowest cost per card.
