Complete guide

NFC business cards: the last business card you’ll need to order

Paper business cards have a short life. They get lost in pockets, go out of date the moment your phone number or job title changes, and most of them end up in a drawer. An NFC business card fixes all three problems at once: you keep the card, the other person gets your details on their phone, and you can update those details whenever you want.

Every Taploe card includes an editable digital profile. Add your phone, email, WhatsApp, website, booking link and social profiles. When someone taps, their phone opens your profile with a button to save your contact. Nothing to install, on iPhone or Android.

Why a QR code too?

Most modern phones read NFC, but not everyone knows where to tap. The printed QR code gives people a second, familiar option, so no connection is ever lost.
